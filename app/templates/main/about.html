Our Story

The journey of Maagi Pickle, by Prashanth Home Industries.

Our Story

Maagi Pickle was founded in 2001 by Prashanth Home Industries with a single goal: to bring the authentic taste of traditional Indian pickles to every home. What began as a small home kitchen operation has grown steadily, while never losing sight of the recipes and care that started it all.

Our Philosophy

We believe good pickle-making cannot be rushed. Each batch follows time-tested methods — the right balance of spice, oil and sun, prepared patiently rather than mass-produced.

Quality Commitment

From ingredient selection to final packing, every step is checked for consistency. We work with trusted local suppliers for our raw produce and spices.

Traditional Preparation

Our pickles are prepared using traditional techniques — hand-cut vegetables, slow-cooked masalas, and time-honoured curing methods that give Maagi Pickle its distinct taste.

Packaging & Hygiene

We pack our products in a clean, controlled environment to preserve freshness and ensure every jar that reaches you is just as good as the one before it.

Business Expansion

As demand for Maagi Pickle has grown, we've expanded from direct retail to serving restaurants, hotels, retailers, wholesalers and distributors across India. We continue to invest in our processes so we can grow without compromising on the quality our customers expect.
